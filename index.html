How to Use

1 Buy a Bluetooth thermal printer
We used the Phomemo M02 — it's cheap and does the job. Make sure you buy sticky thermal paper that is black on white. The paper that comes with the M02 is transparent and not black which has mixed results.
2 Connect the printer
Switch the printer on, then tap Connect at the top of this page and pick it from the Bluetooth list. This talks to the printer directly — no Phomemo app needed. It only works in Chrome on Android (or desktop Chrome/Edge); other browsers don't support Web Bluetooth.
3 Generate and print
Tap a mana value to generate a random creature, then hit Print. The card goes straight to the printer in one tap. If Bluetooth isn't available, Share still renders the card as an image for the Phomemo app.
Janky and simple. Have fun!
