How to Use

1 Buy a Bluetooth thermal printer
We used the Phomemo M02 — it's cheap and does the job. Make sure you buy sticky thermal paper that is black on white. The paper that comes with the M02 is transparent and not black which has mixed results.
2 Pair printer and set up the app
Pair the printer to your phone via Bluetooth. Download the printer's app (e.g. Phomemo app) and go through the setup process.
3 Generate and share
Tap a mana value to generate a random creature. Hit Share — this renders a thermal-printer-friendly version of the card and opens your phone's share sheet. Select the printer app, which will handle the print from there.
Janky and simple. Have fun!
Designed for mobile use. Only tested on Android using Chrome.
